## Design Context

### Users
Curious readers exploring what happens to workers as AI makes their jobs obsolete. They need to compare employer obligations, government support and taxes without prior economics training. Technical readers can inspect the assumptions, equations and results.

### Brand Personality
Plain, careful and calm. Explain what the model computes and where its assumptions matter. Follow the user's preference for concise prose without AI writing mannerisms.

### Aesthetic Direction
Keep the existing light editorial appearance, serif headings, warm paper background and restrained green/rust accents. The user explicitly chose a concise tool with technical details expandable. Avoid decorative metrics, repeated explanations and unnecessary controls.

### Design Principles
1. Put the worker outcome and policy choices before implementation detail.
2. Separate employer pay, public support and household taxes visibly.
3. Distinguish US majority stability from the foreign actor's best response; never label an unstable fallback an equilibrium.
4. Keep core controls accessible, with technical assumptions and diagnostics in disclosures.
5. Label observed economic reference values separately from scenario assumptions; preserve keyboard access, contrast and a responsive interface during calculation.
